Maple Mono NF v7.9 / Nerd Fonts glyph and license notices
==========================================================

Maple's original v7.9 release config.json explicitly selects Nerd Fonts 3.4.0 with --complete. Its original font license and authors are ../LICENSE.txt and ../AUTHORS.txt. Nerd Fonts patch copyright and both MIT/OFL license texts are in NERD-FONTS-LICENSE.txt. Maple-NF is distributed unchanged; the patching and glyph modification happened upstream.
